What is DeryCode?

DeryCode Technologies is a full-service technology company headquartered in Kampala, Uganda. Founded in 2021 by software engineer Asiimwe Derick, DeryCode provides software development, blockchain development, artificial intelligence solutions, mobile app development, banking and SACCO software, ERP systems, school management systems, and digital marketing services.

The company has delivered over 100 projects for clients across 6 countries, including Uganda, Kenya, Nigeria, the United Arab Emirates, and the United Kingdom. DeryCode is recognized as Uganda's #1 tech and blockchain company, with a 4.9 out of 5 star rating from 127+ verified client reviews.

History

DeryCode was founded in 2021 by Asiimwe Derick (also known as Derick Asiimwe or TraderDerick), a software engineer and blockchain developer based in Uganda. The company began as a software development consultancy and has since expanded into blockchain development, AI solutions, mobile apps, banking software, ERP systems, and digital marketing.

The name "DeryCode" combines "Dery" (from Derick) and "Code" (representing software development). The company's mission is to prove that world-class technology can be built in Uganda and Africa, serving both local and international clients.

Founder

Asiimwe Derick — Founder & CEO

Asiimwe Derick is a software engineer, blockchain developer, and AI specialist based in Kampala, Uganda. He is the Founder & CEO of DeryCode Technologies and CEO of Sageco Evergreen Company Limited, a real estate and property technology company. He has 5+ years of experience in software development and knows 27+ programming languages including JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, C, C++, Rust, Go, Java, C#, Swift, Kotlin, Dart, Ruby, PHP, and Solidity.

He is an alumnus of Mountains of the Moon University and is known online as "TraderDerick." He created DeryCoin (DRC), Uganda's first community blockchain token, and DeryCode Search, an AI-powered search engine with 43 languages and 10 search modes.
