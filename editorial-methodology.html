DeryCode Editorial Methodology

At DeryCode, we hold our content to high standards. This page explains how we research, write, and maintain our technical articles and guides.

Our Editorial Standards

We believe that technical content should be accurate, practical, and written for real people — not just for search engines. Our editorial methodology ensures every article we publish meets these standards.

Our Editorial Principles

Accuracy

All technical claims are verified and based on real experience.

Local Relevance

Content is tailored to the Ugandan and East African context.

Timeliness

Content is reviewed and updated regularly.

Expert Authorship

Articles are written by developers with real experience.

Transparency

We cite sources and disclose limitations.

Practical Value

Every article provides actionable, useful information.

Research Process

Before writing an article, we research the topic using official documentation, practical development experience, and industry best practices. We test code examples and verify technical claims.

Review Process

Every article goes through technical review by at least one other team member. We check for accuracy, clarity, and completeness.

Update Process

Technology changes fast. We review our articles every 6 months and update them with new information, tools, or best practices.

Content Guidelines

We write for humans first. We avoid keyword stuffing, AI-generated filler content, and thin pages designed only to rank. Every page must provide genuine value to the reader.
