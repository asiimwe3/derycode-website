The Challenge with Software in Uganda

Many Ugandan businesses struggle with off-the-shelf software that doesn't fit their workflows, manual processes that slow growth, and systems that can't integrate with local payment methods like MTN MoMo and Airtel Money. Custom software solves these problems by being built specifically for your business needs and the Ugandan operating environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does software development cost in Uganda?

Most projects take 4–12 weeks. Simple apps can be built in 2–4 weeks, while enterprise systems may take 3–6 months.

Yes, we have extensive experience integrating MTN MoMo API, Airtel Money API, and Pesapal for Ugandan payment processing.
