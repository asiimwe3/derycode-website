Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

DeryCode ("we", "us", "our") builds websites, mobile apps, and software platforms for clients across Uganda and East Africa. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, use our applications, or engage us for services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit via our booking, contact, or newsletter forms — name, email, phone number, and project details.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited, device type, and browser, collected via standard web analytics.
  • Any information you share with us directly over WhatsApp, email, or phone during project discussions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide project quotes.
  • To deliver, support, and maintain software we build for you.
  • To send occasional updates if you subscribe to our newsletter (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To improve our website and services based on how they're used.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted third-party services strictly necessary to deliver our services — for example, payment processors (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, PesaPal), hosting providers, and communication tools like WhatsApp Business.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including secure hosting, access controls, and encrypted connections (HTTPS) across our web properties.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us at any time by contacting us at info@derycode.com or via WhatsApp.

6. Cookies

Our website may use minimal cookies or local storage for functionality such as theme preference and form handling. We do not use invasive tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, reach out to us at info@derycode.com or on WhatsApp at +256 772 002 326.
