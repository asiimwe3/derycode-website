The organisation

Western Youth Tech Foundation.

Talent is everywhere in western Uganda. Opportunity is not, and neither is a machine to learn on. The foundation puts young people in front of one for free: coding, cyber-safety, and how to earn and save online without being robbed by a stranger on a phone. The best of them leave with paid work, not a certificate. Fund one seat and you change a household.

WHAT IT DOES

Free training, in person

Coding, computer basics and online earning, taught in Kyenjojo and the surrounding districts. Cohorts are small because the machines are shared.

WHY IT MATTERS

Cyber-safety and money

How to spot online scams and how to save and send money safely on a phone. Most learners meet the internet for the first time on a borrowed handset.

WHERE IT LEADS

A job, not a certificate

The best graduates go on to paid work with the studio or with the businesses we serve. That is the whole point of the foundation.

Straight answers

What people ask before they write to us

Who owns the products?

DeryCode Technologies owns and operates all eight. Schools and shops license them; they do not buy them and they do not host them on someone else's cloud by default.

Can I buy one product without a project?

Yes. DeryAccount, School Report Maker and DeryCode Search are self-serve: licence, install, work. The ERP and the SACCO wallet are installed with training because they touch money.

Where does the data live?

On your own machine for DeryAccount and School Report Maker. Cloud accounts are optional and are only used for backups and marks bundles. No learner or customer data is sold, and none is used to train models.

Do the products work without internet?

The till, the school console and the teacher app are offline-first by design: a shop in a trading centre and a school off the tarmac use them the same way. Search and the market need a connection, and say so.

Is there an IDE or developer tool coming?

Yes, in development. DeryCode Studio is a coding environment we are building for our own work first, because the editors we use assume fast networks. No public beta and no release date yet.

Which one do you want to see working?

Tell us the shop, the school or the SACCO. We will show you the product running on real data, not a slide.
