Where AI actually sits

We use AI the way a workshop uses a power tool.

Three places in our products use machine learning. Each one does a narrow job, on the device where the work happens, and never with a child's record going to somebody else's server.

01

Reading a photographed marksheet

A teacher photographs the paper class list and the phone reads the handwritten scores into the system. The model runs on the phone, so a school with no data bundle still gets it.

02

Answers in DerySearch

Our own search engine answers a question in plain language and cites the sources it used, in English, Swahili, Luganda, Runyankole or Ateso.

03

An assistant on WhatsApp

Quotes, availability and follow-ups are handled by an assistant on our WhatsApp line. A human reads anything it is unsure about before it reaches you.

What we will not do

  • No learner data in training. Marks, names and guardians stay in the school's own file.
  • No fake AI labels. If a screen does not use a model, it does not say AI on it.
  • No unreviewed output. Comments and reports are drafted, then read by the teacher who signs them.
  • Offline first. Every AI feature degrades to a manual path, because a school 200km from a tarmac road has the same deadline as one in the Gulf.

Questions

The ones asked before every project starts.

How much does a website cost at DeryCode?

A business website starts at UGX 500,000 for a starter site, UGX 1,200,000 for the pro build and UGX 2,500,000 and above for a custom design. Larger corporate sites run to UGX 4,500,000. The full figure for each service is in the price table above and on the price list page.

How long does a project take?

A business website takes 1 to 3 weeks. A school system or POS takes 3 to 6 weeks. A SACCO or banking system takes 6 to 12 weeks, depending on branches and how clean the old records are.

Do you work with clients outside Africa?

Yes. Engineering sits in Uganda and delivery is remote-first, so we work with clients across Africa, the Gulf and Europe. We overlap working hours with the Gulf and Europe, quote in USD or UGX, and travel for go-live and training where the project needs it.

Do I own the website and the data?

Yes. The domain, hosting account and source code are handed over in your name at launch. School and SACCO data stays in your own database, and we never use learner or member records for anything else.

Can you take mobile money payments?

Yes. We integrate MTN MoMo and Airtel Money collection APIs, and card payments through PesaPal or Flutterwave where a business needs them.

What do you need from me to start?

Your existing paperwork, a few real photographs, and one person who can approve work. We agree a written scope and a fixed price, then 40 percent starts the build.

Do you use AI in the software?

In three narrow places: reading a photographed marksheet on the phone, answering questions inside DerySearch, and handling first replies on our WhatsApp line. Nothing else carries an AI label, and learner data is never used to train a model.
