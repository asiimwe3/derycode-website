Why DeryCode

Claims you can check, not adjectives.

We are a small studio, so we rely on evidence instead of scale. Each point below can be verified on this site, in a public repository, or by visiting a business that runs the software.

The till, the hotel ledger and the foundation are operated by us and by family businesses every day. Software that fails here gets fixed before a client ever sees it.

Offline first, not offline optional

The till, the school console and the teacher app are built to work with the network switched off, because that is the normal condition, not the exception.

Your data stays on your machine

DeryAccount and School Report Maker keep the school and shop data locally. Cloud accounts are optional, used for backups and marks bundles only.

Shipped in small, real releases

The organisation

Western Youth Tech Foundation.

Talent is everywhere in western Uganda. Opportunity is not, and neither is a machine to learn on. The foundation puts young people in front of one for free: coding, cyber-safety, and how to earn and save online without being robbed by a stranger on a phone. The best of them leave with paid work, not a certificate.

Free training, in person

Coding, computer basics and online earning, taught in Kyenjojo and the surrounding districts. Cohorts stay small because the machines are shared.

Cyber-safety and money

How to spot online scams, and how to save and send money safely on a phone, because most learners meet the internet on a borrowed handset.

A job, not a certificate

The strongest graduates go on to paid work with the studio or with the businesses we serve. That is the whole point of the foundation.

Straight answers

The questions buyers actually ask.

If your question is not here, the answers library has 59 more, or ask us on WhatsApp.

Do I have to buy a project to use a product?

No. DeryAccount, School Report Maker and DeryCode Search are self-serve: licence, install, work. The hotel ERP and the SACCO wallet are installed with training because they touch money.

What happens when the internet goes off?

The till, the school console and the teacher app are offline-first by design, and keep working with no connection at all. DeryCode Search and DeryMarket need a connection and say so plainly.

Where does my data live?

On your own machine for DeryAccount and School Report Maker. Cloud accounts are optional, used only for backups and marks bundles. No learner or customer data is sold, and none of it is used to train models.

Who owns and supports these products?

DeryCode owns and operates all of them. Support comes from the engineer who built the product, on one WhatsApp number, and we also run the systems inside our own businesses.

Is there an IDE or developer tool?

Yes, in development. DeryCode Studio is a coding environment built for our own work first, because the editors we use assume fast networks. No public beta and no release date yet.
