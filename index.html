THE PROBLEM

Paper, guesswork and disconnected systems are costing African businesses.

Manual Work

Staff spend hours every day doing by hand what software should do — copying, calculating, retyping. Time that should grow the business is lost to paperwork.

Lost Information

Records live in exercise books, spreadsheets and people's heads. When a file is lost or a staff member leaves, the organization's memory leaves with them.

Disconnected Systems

One tool for fees, another for records, nothing talks to anything. Every report becomes a manual puzzle — and decisions wait on data nobody trusts.

DeryCode's answer: one connected system per organization — software that works offline, automates the manual work, keeps every record safe, and gives leaders trusted numbers on demand.
