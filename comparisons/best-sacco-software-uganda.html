Choosing the Right SACCO Software

With many SACCO software options available in Uganda, choosing the right one for your cooperative is critical. This comparison helps SACCO leaders evaluate options based on features, pricing, and local support.

What to Look for in SACCO Software

MoMo Integration

MTN MoMo and Airtel Money deposit/repayment support.

Offline Support

Works without internet — critical for rural SACCOs.

Member Management

Member registration, KYC, and profiles.

Loan Management

Full loan lifecycle from application to repayment.

Shares & Dividends

Share capital and dividend calculation.

Financial Reports

Balance sheets, P&L, and regulatory reports.

Key Factors When Choosing SACCO Software

When evaluating SACCO software in Uganda, consider: (1) Does it support mobile money integration? (2) Can it work offline? (3) Is it affordable for your SACCO size? (4) Does the vendor offer local support? (5) Can it scale as your SACCO grows? (6) Does it meet regulatory requirements from the Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best SACCO software in Uganda?

The best SACCO software depends on your SACCO's size, location, and needs. Key features to look for include MoMo integration, offline support, loan management, and local vendor support.

How much does SACCO software cost in Uganda?

SACCO software costs range from UGX 3,800,000 for basic systems to UGX 15,000,000+ for enterprise solutions. Monthly maintenance typically costs UGX 500,000-1,500,000.
