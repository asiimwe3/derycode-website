How it works

1. Register your institution and set up your loan products. 2. Loan officers capture clients and applications from the field — online or offline. 3. Managers approve, disburse and monitor the portfolio. 4. Dena keeps the books, flags arrears and produces the reports your board and regulators ask for.

Roadmap to launch

PHASE 1

Core engine

Loan products, client records, disbursements, collections and the accounting core.

PHASE 2

Field operations

Loan-officer app with offline collection, savings accounts and mobile money reconciliation.

Pilot with Ugandan SACCOs and money lenders, then general availability with subscriptions.
